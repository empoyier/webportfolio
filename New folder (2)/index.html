Our story

A family mission that turns a small gift into a joyful memory.

How it started

Project Smile began in 2016 in Zambales as a yearly family project led by Rey E. Dalusong, built on the belief that a simple treat can brighten a child's day in a lasting way.

What we give

Donations help us prepare Jollibee yum burgers and juice drinks as a special budget treat for children who seldom get the chance to enjoy them.

Who makes it possible

This mission is supported by relatives, friends, and kindhearted donors who want to be part of creating smiles in the community every year.
